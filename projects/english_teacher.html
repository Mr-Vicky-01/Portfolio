THE IDEA

Project overview

An AI language assistant that turns Tamil or Tanglish queries into grammatically correct English. Google’s generative AI and Hugging Face models support translation and spoken output.

What it does

  • Enter a query in Tamil or Tanglish.
  • Generate a grammatically correct English sentence.
  • Listen to the translated sentence with text-to-speech.
