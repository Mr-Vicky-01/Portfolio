THE IDEA

Project overview

An image-to-story pipeline that combines image captioning, language generation, and text-to-speech. Hugging Face Transformers and LangChain turn visual input into short stories that can also be heard.

What it does

  • Generate a caption from an input image.
  • Expand the caption into a short story.
  • Convert the generated story into audio.
